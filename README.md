# Privacy Policy for My Docs: PDF Tools

**Last updated:** June 29, 2026

**My Docs: PDF Tools** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information our application collects, how it is used, and how your data is protected.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information

My Docs: PDF Tools does not collect, process, or store personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address. Most features work completely offline.

### Documents and Files

Documents including PDF files, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, images, and other supported files are processed locally on your device.

We do not upload, store, or access your documents on our servers. Files are only shared externally when you intentionally use your device's Share feature or save files to a location you choose.

### Device Information

Some third-party services used by the app may automatically collect limited device information, including:

* Advertising ID
* Device identifiers
* Crash reports
* Performance and diagnostic information

This information helps deliver advertisements, improve app stability, and fix technical issues.

---

## 2. Permissions We Request

### Storage Access

My Docs: PDF Tools requires storage access to discover, open, edit, convert, and manage documents stored on your device.

This permission allows the app to:

* Scan your device for supported document types
* Display documents in an organized list
* Open PDF, Word, Excel, RTF, and image files
* Save newly created or edited documents
* Import files selected by you

Your original files are never uploaded to our servers.

Where possible, edited documents are saved as new files or to a location selected by you using the Android system file picker.

### Camera

Camera permission is required for:

* Document scanning
* OCR (Text Recognition)
* Image to PDF conversion

Photos captured within the app are processed locally on your device.

### Internet

Internet access is used only for:

* Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob
* Downloading optional machine learning models used by Google ML Kit when required
* Checking for app updates or online services if available

Your personal documents are never uploaded to our servers.

---

## 3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used only to provide the app's functionality, including:

* Viewing PDF documents
* Editing supported documents
* Converting files to PDF
* Compressing, merging, splitting, and resizing PDFs
* OCR text recognition
* Managing and organizing files
* Improving application stability and performance
* Displaying advertisements

We do not sell your personal information or document data.

## 5. Data Security

Your documents remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them.

We implement reasonable security practices to protect the information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
